About Us

At Fibonacci Films, we believe in the power of storytelling to connect, inspire, and inform. For over 25 years, we've been delivering meaningful entertainment and infotainment to Indian households through high-quality audio-visual content. From compelling documentaries and award-winning short films to engaging audio series and ad films, our work spans across genres and platforms. Our mission is to craft stories that reflect both local flavor and global perspectives.

Our Expertise

Global Reach

Our Presence

Headquartered in Mumbai, Fibonacci Films has production facilities across Kolkata, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, and an international presence in London.

The Team

The Fibonacci Films team comprises a dynamic blend of professionals with decades of experience across the film and media industry. Our core team includes alumni from prestigious film institutes and experts in mass communication, along with skilled cinematographers, sound engineers, post-production supervisors, and dubbing coordinators. Their collective experience spans a broad spectrum—from feature films and government-sponsored productions for organizations like Doordarshan, Films Division, NFDC, and various ministries, to corporate films, OTT video and audio series, and short-form digital content. With deep knowledge of both traditional and contemporary storytelling formats, our team is dedicated to delivering high-caliber content that meets the creative and technical standards of modern media consumption.
